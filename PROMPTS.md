The three refresh timestamps shown above were generated locally when saving each retrieved result; they are recorded as `retrieval_recorded_at_utc`, not as actual capture times. The desktop and mobile captures have no supplied retrieval-record timestamp. Local file modification times are separately labeled.

- DOM presence does not imply that every prompt was simultaneously visible in the screenshot; entries may be collapsed, clipped, or below the viewport.
- Provenance uses the source site, capture IDs, original HTML SHA-256 hashes, and ordered observations recorded in `refresh-log.json`. This catalog is complete without the internal raw capture files.
